# ADR-002: ADR format and content

ADRs live in `/.adrs/` at the repository root and follow the naming convention
`NNNN-short-title.md` (e.g. `0001-scoped-custom-element-registration.md`). They are kept out
of the published docs site — they record _why_ a decision was made, not user-facing guidance.

## Required sections

Every ADR must include:

1. **Title** — the decision in imperative form (e.g. "Register components through a scoped
   registry")
2. **Date** — when the decision was made (YYYY-MM-DD)
3. **Context** — the problem or situation motivating the decision; include constraints,
   requirements, and alternatives considered
4. **Decision** — what we decided to do; be specific and actionable
5. **Consequences** — both positive and negative outcomes; include migration burden,
   maintenance cost, and what we're giving up

## Writing guidelines

- **Be concise** — one page is ideal; if longer, the decision may need splitting
- **Focus on "why"** — the code shows "what"; the ADR explains "why"
- **Name alternatives** — even if briefly; helps readers understand the decision space
- **Link to context** — reference issues, changesets, or discussions that informed the
  decision
- **Cross-reference rules** — if a decision is codified as a rule (e.g. `CHARM-002`), link to
  it so the "why" and the "what" stay connected
